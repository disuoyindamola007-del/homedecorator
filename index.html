About Us

We turn houses into homes people are proud of.

Adara Interiors began with one living room and a simple belief: your home should look beautiful and feel easy to live in. Twelve years on, that belief still guides every project we take.

We have decorated homes, apartments, offices and event venues all over Nigeria, from Lagos and Abuja to Port Harcourt, Enugu, Ibadan, Kano and beyond. Each job starts with listening. We learn how you live, what you love and what you want to come home to.

Then we take care of everything: colours, furniture, lighting, curtains and the small finishing touches that make a room feel complete. You get a space that feels like you, delivered on time and on budget.

Adaeze OkonkwoFounder and Creative Director
